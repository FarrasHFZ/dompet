How the score works

Invest = cube root of (momentum x quality x durability), capped at 59 if brand safety is flagged. Multiplying means one weak link drags the whole score down.

PartBuilt from
MomentumLoudness of recent stories (outlets, up to 6) x freshness (14-day decay). Once 7+ days of follower snapshots exist, it is averaged with follower growth rate (full marks at 0.6% a day).
QualityWeighted by event type: content/collab 1.0, commercial 0.9, event 0.7, personal 0.4, conflict 0.2, legal 0.1.
Durability25 base + 20 per dated catalyst in the next 14 days + 15 per recent content/collab/commercial event + 10 if 30-day growth is positive.
Brand safetyFlag if there is a legal or conflict story with 3+ outlets in the last 90 days.
Verdict70+ and safe: Invest now. 50-69: Prepare. 35-49: Watch. Below 35: Hold. Falling followers on all platforms: Reduce.
ConfidenceLow under 7 days of snapshots. Medium 7-13 days. High 14+ days and 3+ events.

These weights are first-pass heuristics, not fitted to outcomes. Tune them against real campaign results once conversion data is connected.
