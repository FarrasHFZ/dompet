How the score works

Everyone is tracked on follower snapshots (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube). Only creators with an event scan get an Invest score, because the score needs news and event evidence that public follower counts cannot give.

Invest = cube root of (momentum x quality x durability), capped at 59 if brand safety is flagged. Multiplying means one weak link drags the whole score down.

MomentumLoudness of recent stories (outlets, up to 6) x freshness (14-day decay). Once 7+ days of snapshots exist, averaged with follower growth rate (full marks at 0.6% a day).
QualityWeighted by event type: content/collab 1.0, commercial 0.9, event 0.7, personal 0.4, conflict 0.2, legal 0.1. With no events found it sits at 50.
Durability25 base + 20 per dated catalyst in the next 14 days + 15 per recent content/collab/commercial event + 10 if 30-day growth is positive.
Brand safetyFlag if there is a legal or conflict story with 3+ outlets in the last 90 days.
Verdict70+ and safe: Invest now. 50-69: Prepare. 35-49: Watch. Below 35: Hold. Falling followers on every platform: Reduce.
Follower status (everyone)Rising: 7-day growth of 0.4%/day or more, or 2x the 30-day pace. Cooling: shrinking over 7 and 30 days. Collecting until 7 days of snapshots exist.
ConfidenceLow under 7 days of snapshots. Medium 7-13 days. High 14+ days and 3+ events. Auto-scanned creators stay at low.

Weights are first-pass heuristics, not fitted to outcomes. Instagram shows exact counts; TikTok and YouTube round to three digits. Tune against campaign results once sales data is connected.
